    Installation

    1. Download Starseer_1.8.9.zip. Don't unzip it.
    2. Open Lunar or Badlion on 1.8.9 and go to Options → Resource Packs → Open Pack Folder.
    3. Move the ZIP into that folder (it's %APPDATA%\.minecraft\resourcepacks).
    4. Back in game, move the pack to the right-hand column and click Done.
    5. To see the sky and connected glass, turn on Custom Sky, Connected Textures and Animated Textures in your client settings.
    1. Install OptiFine for 1.8.9 (the pack works without it, but with no custom sky, connected glass or animated enchanting letters).
    2. Press Win + R, type %APPDATA%\.minecraft\resourcepacks and press Enter.
    3. Copy Starseer_1.8.9.zip there.
    4. In game: Options → Resource Packs, enable the pack and click Done.
    5. In Options → Video Settings → Quality: set Connected Textures to Fancy and Custom Sky to ON. In Animations, set everything to ON.

    Help

    Does it lower FPS?

    No. It's a 16×16 pack, the same resolution as vanilla. The custom sky and connected glass rely on OptiFine and cost very little.

    Does it work on Hypixel and other servers?

    Yes. It's a resource pack: it only changes how things look on your screen and gives no unfair advantage.

    I can't see the sky or the connected glass

    You need OptiFine (or Lunar or Badlion, which include it) with Custom Sky and Connected Textures turned on. Then reload with F3 + T.

    Can I use it in my videos or streams?

    Of course. A link to this page in your description is appreciated.

    Can I edit or reupload it?

    You can edit it for personal use. Don't reupload it to other sites or sell it. Share a link to this page instead.

    Why does version 26.3 have fewer textures?

    It's being ported in batches. For now it includes wool, glass, defense blocks, weapons, tools and shop items.
